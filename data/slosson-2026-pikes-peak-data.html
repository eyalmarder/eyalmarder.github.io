Historic climate, cosmogenic ¹⁰Be, denudation-rate, and geospatial datasets from the Pikes Peak region, Colorado, USA

A collection of climate, cosmogenic beryllium-10, denudation-rate, and geospatial data associated with research in the Pikes Peak region of Colorado.

Open data record

What the dataset covers

  • Historic climate data
  • Cosmogenic beryllium-10
  • Denudation rates
  • Geospatial data

Using the data

Data and documentation are available in ESS-DIVE. The associated manuscript is in revision for Geology.
