The study behind the data.

Geochronology · 2026

Meteoric beryllium-10 fluxes from soil inventory measurements in the East River watershed, Colorado, USA

Soil inventories on dated moraines calibrate how atmospheric beryllium-10 reaches mountain soils, improving its use as a tracer of surface processes in quartz-poor landscapes.
