Elevation-dependent denudation rates on Pikes Peak, Rocky Mountains, USA

Cosmogenic beryllium-10 measurements, climate records, and terrain analysis investigate how denudation varies with elevation on Pikes Peak.

The research question

How do long-term denudation rates vary with elevation on Pikes Peak?

Approach & current work

This collaborative study uses cosmogenic beryllium-10 measurements, climate records, and terrain analysis to examine elevation-dependent denudation on Pikes Peak.

The associated datasets are available in ESS-DIVE.
