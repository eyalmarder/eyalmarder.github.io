Drainage networks on Earth and Mars

I compare drainage networks on Earth and Mars to test what their geometry can tell us about runoff where direct flow measurements are unavailable.

The question

Can the arrangement of tributaries and contributing areas help us investigate ancient runoff on Mars?

The approach

I combine channel lengths and contributing areas in a network geometry index, compare drainage organization across Earth and Mars, and test its relationship with measured mean runoff on Earth. The work also examines how calibration choices and geographic differences affect any transfer to Mars.
