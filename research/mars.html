In review at JGR: Planets

A whole-network view of drainage

Can drainage-network geometry help us investigate runoff where direct measurements are unavailable?

I combine channel lengths with contributing areas to describe the organization of entire drainage networks. The study compares Earth and Martian networks, tests the relationship between geometry and measured mean runoff on Earth, and examines the limits of transferring an Earth calibration to Mars.
