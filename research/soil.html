Reconstructing conservation-era soil movement

How has soil redistribution varied during the conservation era?

Cesium-137 binds to soil particles and records their redistribution since the mid-twentieth century. I combine soil profiles from cultivated fields and native-prairie reference sites with LiDAR terrain measurements and a soil-transport model to investigate local surface lowering and its relationship with relief.

Calibrating a tracer in mountain soils

How much atmospheric beryllium-10 reaches mountain soils?

Soil inventories on dated East River moraines calibrate atmospheric beryllium-10 delivery across an elevation gradient. This local calibration improves the use of the tracer to investigate surface processes, including in quartz-poor landscapes.
