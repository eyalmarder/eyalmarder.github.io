Science · 2025

Mountain building and biodiversity

Coupled simulations explore how rising mountains create opportunities for biological diversification in highlands and neighboring basins.

Journal article

The question

How do the pace and magnitude of mountain uplift shape biodiversity as landscapes evolve?

How we approached it

We coupled landscape evolution with a biological model of small-mammal dispersal, adaptation, and speciation, then compared outcomes across uplift scenarios.

What we found

  • Greater uplift rates and magnitudes increased simulated biodiversity.
  • Changing topography generated habitats and geographic isolation that supported diversification.
  • Highland species also spread into adjacent depositional lowlands.

Why it matters

Mountain building can shape biological diversity beyond mountain boundaries, linking tectonics to the evolution of surrounding ecosystems.

Cited in a UNEP assessment · 2026

Mountains in Motion: Global Linkages from Ridge to River (p. 15). The assessment cites this study’s finding that mountain building can generate biodiversity in highlands and neighboring basins.
